What we do

Halif Media represents a roster of influencers and pitches them to brands we believe are genuinely the right fit — not just the highest bidder. We handle the matchmaking, the pitch, and the negotiation, and we take a percentage of the deals we bring in.

The goal isn't a one-off post. It's a relationship between an influencer and a brand that both sides want to repeat.

  • Honesty is the brand We tell influencers and brands the truth even when it costs us a deal. That's what makes the next deal possible.
